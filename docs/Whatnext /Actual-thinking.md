- Duplicate detection and reconciliation provide measurable value.
- GST and bank workflows can reuse the same extraction, storage and validation engine.

For your initial development, you can run most of this locally on your Ubuntu machine. For live CA documents, use an appropriately secured deployment and storage setup, not an exposed development machine.

I would also avoid purchasing a large VPS just because an advertisement recommends one. First benchmark your OCR and LLM workloads. CPU-based inference may need a different deployment strategy from normal API hosting.

### What to do when you enter a CA office

Don't immediately pitch the product.

Say something like:

Hello, I'm working on KnowledgeHub, a document management and AI processing platform specifically designed for CA firms.

I'm trying to understand how firms currently manage client documents, invoice processing, document verification and related office tasks.

I'd like to understand your existing workflow and identify repetitive work that could be automated. I'm also developing a working solution and would appreciate an opportunity to demonstrate it once the initial version is ready.

Could I speak with the person who manages your firm's document-processing workflow?

During the visit, try to learn:

- How documents arrive at the office.
- How they organize documents by client and financial year.
- Which PDF operations they perform frequently.
- How much time staff spend entering invoice data.
- How they identify missing documents.
- Which software they already use.
- Which tasks are still done manually.

Ask to see a demonstration of their existing process, not confidential documents.

# 8. Pilot pricing and commercial model

Because the product will cover more than invoice extraction, eventually you can sell it as a document operations platform.

But initially, keep pricing tied to usage and support.

| Offering                  | Suggested starting price |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Initial 14-day pilot      | ₹7,500                   |
| Starter monthly plan      | ₹4,999                   |
| Growth monthly plan       | ₹9,999                   |
| Professional monthly plan | ₹17,999                  |
| Custom integration        | Separately quoted        |

The pilot should include the core document management features and one specialized invoice-processing workflow, with agreed usage limits.

Don't promise unlimited PDF processing, AI usage or document storage at these prices. Measure infrastructure and support costs before finalizing the plans.

## One important business decision

I would position KnowledgeHub as a document operations platform for professional firms, beginning with CA firms.

That means CA firms are your first market, not your permanent product limitation. Later, the same core platform can support:

- Insurance claims document processing.
- Legal case-file management.
- Loan application document verification.
- Financial institution document workflows.
- General enterprise document management.

You build the reusable engine once and configure workflows for different industries.

My recommendation: spend the first week visiting CA firms while designing the data model and MVP. Do not spend eight weeks coding in isolation and only then discover that their biggest problem is different from what you assumed.

The next useful deliverable is a complete KnowledgeHub product specification: screen-by-screen frontend modules, backend APIs, database tables, processing pipeline, permission model and development task breakdown. That will turn this plan into an implementation blueprint.
