Ask each business the same basic questions:

1. What types of documents does your business receive?
2. Where do they arrive from?
3. How are they organized?
4. Which documents require manual data entry?
5. Which information must be checked against other documents?
6. What causes delays, mistakes or repeated work?
7. Which tasks consume the most employee time?
8. What software do you already use?
9. Would you pay to automate one of these tasks?

Don't just ask whether they want AI. Ask them to show you the actual workflow.
