Questions

Do my client documents go to the internet?

No. Documents, OCR and the database stay on your computer. Only the login (email, password, computer ID) is checked online.

Do I need internet all the time?

Only for a few seconds when you open the app. After that it works offline for up to 24 hours.

How many computers can I use?

Each plan includes a number of computers; you can add more. You can see and remove computers in your account.

Is there a free trial?

Contact us for a guided trial on your own invoices.
